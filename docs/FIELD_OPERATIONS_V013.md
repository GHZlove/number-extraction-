# Field Operations · v0.14.0

The browser playtest extends the existing expedition systems. Launch the extracted archive with `start-windows.bat`.

| Input | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Z · ×2 | Ten seconds of doubled combat/gate number and −25% move speed. Temporary forms cannot merge or raise the saved best number. |
| X · √ | Set both numbers to the lower midpoint tier between their binary exponents. A blocked attempt keeps the operator. |
| V · LOCK | Eight seconds of no healing, division, fusion or summons for that enemy. It can still take damage. |
| T · Rift | At permanent 2048, spend one tier on a shock across the visible screen, respecting fog and cover. Bosses take capped damage. |

Remnant collection grows at 16. At 128 a one-hit shield recharges after 22 seconds. At 512 nearby lower numbers slow down. Prime elites resist division. Radix shields block direct hits and can be stripped by Q. Tagged fusion enemies can combine with nearby equal-number allies after a visible countdown.

The eight two-slot interactions in [the design notes](SYNERGIES_V012_DESIGN.md) use the existing attack cooldown. Their icons light when an eligible target is primed. Fire can consume remnants in small blasts. Wind can launch them into a nearby foe. Purple and red weapons can carry a prototype trait. The heat-barrel trait trades heat for fire rate. Lightweight weapons trade the armour slot for speed.

Broadcasts rotate roughly every 60–90 seconds. The minimap marks airdrops and roaming smugglers, and outlines the sectors covered by magnetic storms and cold fronts. Smugglers exchange spare weapons for prototype gear or operators. Nitrogen canisters leave a slow zone. High-voltage boxes stun enemies. Wetlands and icefields feature single-direction crossings. Timed and boss extraction routes can alert a small patrol during the extraction hold.

Enter the firing range from the operations or workshop tab. It copies the planned loadout for 20 seconds of practice. The range shows the last attack's total damage, impact distance, weapon range and the available swap window. Esc returns early. Shelter and journal progression do not change. The heart icon enables a quiet heat pulse separately from sound effects. The game honours the browser's reduced-motion preference for ambient movement.
