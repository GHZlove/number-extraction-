# Core Siege · v0.14.0

Choose Core Siege next to the route selector. The shelter issues a copy of the selected loadout. The mode starts at number 2 beside a 500 HP core and records the five best nights on this device. Credits, equipment, and build resources are local to the match.

- Day lasts 30 seconds. Press **E** or click the on-screen **E** button to gather from a well or vein, talk to the trader, or deploy a selected blueprint. The central trader sells blueprints and equipment in daylight.
- Night lasts 45–60 seconds. Enemies enter from the perimeter and navigate toward the core. Proximity or a player hit draws them away. Surviving attackers retreat at dawn.
- Face a clear nearby spot to deploy. Turret, Tesla, mortar and prism towers acquire targets automatically. Place collectors next to number wells, alloy veins or data veins. Crystal nodes passively speed up towers within 250 units. Barricades block paths until destroyed.
- Two adjacent buildings of the same type and number merge when you press **E** nearby. This costs data coins. You can spend alloy to repair structures, or upgrade a full-health core with data and alloy.
- Select a conduit. Press **E** beside a number well to set its source, then beside the core or an attack tower to connect it. A clear line is required. Tiles travel at 90 units per second, and equal adjacent tiles fuse. The core recovers HP from deliveries. Towers gain firing charges. You can still pick up matching tiles in transit.
- Buy a Burn, Frost or Sunder beacon to prime nearby tower hits for Thermal Rupture or Exposed Fault. The player's qualifying hit completes the reaction.

Both modes use the new enemy roles. Incoming corruption lowers only the defender number used in damage calculations and slows movement. A safehouse or phase buff clears it. Two equal enemies can form a link within a shared wave or a commander's field. They must stay close before a 1.5-second warning. Knockback, interrupts, cover, death and LOCK can break a link. Unstoppable units resist ordinary interrupts.

The Windows launcher opens this browser playtest. Music and effects still use the game's independent Web Audio toggles.
