Number Extraction v0.11.1 · Balance Playtest · Windows x64 preview

- A tuning pass before Phase 2: the same maps, weapons, enchantments, warehouse and four run-depth systems, with revised numbers and clearer extraction information.
- Heat grows at 0.24 + 0.02 per difficulty rank above Scout per second. Ordinary kills add 0.2 heat and bosses 5. Hunter intervals are now 40/35/30/25 seconds; the 12 alive/pending and 120 scheduled limits remain. This reduces the kill-to-more-hunters feedback while preserving late-run escalation.
- The 1024–2048 form has radius 26 instead of 28 and 90% walking speed instead of 86%; its 14% damage reduction remains. Fog vision increases from 280 to 360. Volatile remnants warn for 1.3 seconds with radius 85, retaining 15% maximum-health damage.
- Glass Firepower now explicitly increases incoming damage by 25%, alongside 75 maximum health and +35% weapon damage. This fixes the previous interaction where percentage-based enemy damage largely cancelled the intended health tradeoff.
- Special exits open 5–15 seconds after the normal fallback deadline instead of 20–35. Timed windows last 40 seconds in each 70-second cycle. Successful window/key/number/boss extraction adds 8/10/10/15% currency and materials, once at settlement. The western fallback retains its original opening time and rewards; field trading values and equipment are unaffected.
- Extraction intelligence displays the guardian name, key-cache direction/distance/state and each route's reward. The shelter recap includes the earned route bonus, heat and seed for reproducible feedback. Guardian identity remains readable after defeated-enemy cleanup.
- v0.11.0 progress uses the same schema-5 save key and app-data directory: resources, warehouse contents, pending gear and deployment plans are retained. Finish the current raid and close the older running game before opening the new EXE.
- Download `NumberExtraction-0.11.1-Windows-x64.exe`. No Python, Node.js, local server or separate browser is required. F11 / Alt+Enter toggles fullscreen.
- Automated regression and finite-health combat diagnostics accompany the build. Publication requires native Windows startup, seven layout/zoom cases, settings, risk/reward briefing, movement/merging, inventory and restart checks on the final portable EXE. SHA256 and screenshots are attached.
- This is a balance playtest candidate. Automated fixtures do not establish human win rates or comfort. A 2–4 day anonymous playtest guide is included in the repository; outside-player feedback has not yet been collected. Phase 2 is planned for v0.12: equipment synergies and sets, then contracts, then factions.
- This preview is unsigned; Windows may show an unknown-publisher prompt. Keep security protections enabled.
