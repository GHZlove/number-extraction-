# v0.11.1 · Balance candidate and playtest evidence

v0.11.1 tunes the four existing run-depth systems before Phase 2. It aims to make threats more readable, reduce pressure generated by successful combat, improve high-tier movement and give special exits a visible reason to be used. It does not add a new progression system.

**This is a balance candidate, not a completed human balance study.** The finite-health diagnostics below are measured results. Outside-player feedback and the proposed 2–4 day tuning window are still uncollected and must be carried out before claiming that the balance pass is complete. Use the [playtest kit](PLAYTEST_V0111.md) to record those sessions.

## Numeric changes

`rank` is the difficulty rank from 1 to 5. Speeds are walking multipliers relative to the ordinary light form; damage multipliers are applied before the existing defensive calculations.

| Parameter | v0.11.0 | v0.11.1 candidate | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Passive heat per active second | `0.28 + 0.025 × (rank − 1)` | `0.24 + 0.02 × (rank − 1)` | Give exploration more time before escalating pressure, while retaining the timer |
| Ordinary kill heat | 0.4 | 0.2 | Reduce the feedback loop where fighting hunters immediately attracts more pressure |
| Boss kill heat | 8 | 5 | Reduce the extra pressure immediately after a long encounter |
| Hunter intervals, heat levels 1/2/3/4 | 35 / 30 / 25 / 22 seconds | 40 / 35 / 30 / 25 seconds | Add recovery and routing time between squads |
| Core form, numbers 1024–2048 | Radius 28; speed ×0.86 | Radius 26; speed ×0.90 | Reduce collision friction and improve escape movement |
| Core incoming damage | ×0.86 | ×0.86, unchanged | Keep its 14% defensive advantage without adding more power |
| Fog vision radius | 280 units | 360 units | Reveal nearby ranged threats earlier without restoring full vision |
| Volatile death-explosion warning | 0.9 seconds | 1.3 seconds | Give players more time to read the explosion and leave |
| Volatile explosion radius | 95 units | 85 units | Reduce overlap and close-range escape friction |
| Volatile base damage | 15% of player maximum health | 15%, unchanged | Retain a meaningful consequence for ignoring the warning |
| Glass Firepower incoming damage | ×1.00 | ×1.25 | Make the defensive cost affect the game's percentage-based damage |
| Glass Firepower other values | Maximum health 75; direct weapon power ×1.35; reward +10% | Unchanged | Preserve the build's identity while correcting its cost |
| Special-exit first opening | Base difficulty deadline +20–35 seconds | Base deadline +5–15 seconds | Reduce the automatic waiting disadvantage relative to west, without enabling immediate key-card extraction |
| Timed-exit window / period | 30 / 70 seconds | 40 / 70 seconds | Make approaching and completing the channel less brittle |

The Core form remains 10% slower and physically larger than the ordinary light form. Its open-ground walking speed changes from 184.9 to 193.5 units/second at the base speed of 215; terrain and modifier effects still apply. Collision growth still waits for enough space beside a wall. These numbers do not establish that every real player finds 1024 comfortable.

Heat thresholds remain 25/50/75/100. Hunter squad sizes, the combined live/pending cap of 12, the 120 scheduled-arrival budget and the safehouse protections remain in place. Heat still does not decay. The High Bounty multiplier remains ×1.5, so that modifier continues to accelerate pressure.

### Why Glass needed an explicit damage cost

Enemy damage is usually calculated as a fraction of the target's maximum health. Reducing maximum health from 100 to 75 also reduced each such incoming hit proportionally, so the smaller health bar alone did not increase the fraction lost. For example, a nominal 25%-of-maximum-health hit still removed 25% of the bar in either case.

The new ×1.25 incoming multiplier makes that nominal hit remove 31.25% before armour and other defences. Maximum health 75, +35% direct weapon power and the +10% settlement bonus stay unchanged. Healing, armour and other effects can still alter the actual outcome; this change does not guarantee a particular number of hits to defeat every Glass build.

## Extraction rewards and clarity

| Successful extraction route | v0.11.0 route bonus | v0.11.1 route bonus |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Western fallback | 0% | 0% |
| Timed window | 0% | +8% |
| Key card | 0% | +10% |
| Required number | 0% | +10% |
| Guarding boss | 0% | +15% |

The route bonus multiplies successful currency and material settlement once, after the existing difficulty, shelter and additive-mutator multiplier. For example, a reward of 100 before the route multiplier becomes 110 through a key route, subject to normal rounding. It does not change field-trader purchasing power, duplicate equipment, upgrade gear or pay out a route bonus on failure. Existing settlement guards remain responsible for preventing repeat banking.

The western fallback keeps its opening deadline, safety and absence of key/number/boss requirements. Special routes still open after the base deadline, even when a nearby key can be collected quickly. The intent is a choice between a reliable retreat and a nearer or better-paying conditional route, rather than forcing every player to use a special exit.

The extraction briefing exposes the route requirement, timing and reward. Players still need to demonstrate whether they understand these before arriving. Readability changes do not substitute for their feedback.

### Route diagnostic that informed the change

A v0.11.0 route sample performed **48 origin/form searches across 12 worlds**, each checking four exits and the key cache, using an 80-unit, eight-neighbour, terrain-weighted Dijkstra search with collision-radius and line-of-sight checks. No sampled target was unreachable on that grid. Estimated movement times were:

| Route estimate | Minimum | Median | Maximum |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Spawn to special exits, light form | 9.3 s | 27.6 s | 61.0 s |
| Eastern exit back to west, light form | 28.7 s | 41.9 s | 62.2 s |
| Eastern exit back to west, old Core form | 33.4 s | 49.0 s | 72.4 s |
| Spawn to key cache, light form | 5.6 s | 5.7 s | 6.2 s |

These estimates exclude combat, waiting for an opening, dodge, channel time and the work needed to satisfy prerequisites. They are not observed player travel times or a proof of every continuous path. They show that returning west can be a substantial detour, while obtaining the first key can be quick. That supports reducing special-exit waiting and adding modest route rewards while retaining an opening delay. It does not establish that every bonus is worth its risk.

## Paired finite-health combat diagnostics

The same controller was run against the v0.11.0 source snapshot and v0.11.1 candidate: **16 fixtures per version**, with a 60-second observation horizon. Each configuration used seeds 881 and 1777 at starting numbers 64 and 1024. Fixtures used Hard difficulty, eight initial enemies, a white level-1 rifle, no armour or laser, and a 1/30-second fixed step.

The controller used ordinary production movement, attack, dodge, directional parry, limited healing and division inputs. No invulnerability, health replenishment or direct enemy-damage injection was applied during the simulation. Starting numbers and equipment were fixture setup, not earned progression. Baseline and Number Surge/Supply Shortage/Fog started at heat 0; the other two configurations started at heat 55. Compare paired builds within a configuration, not different configurations as if they were human difficulty scores.

| Configuration, four fixtures each | Survived the 60 s horizon, before → after | Mean ending health, % of own maximum, before → after | Mean kills, before → after |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| No modifiers | 4/4 → 4/4 | 76.75 → 78.88 | 20.50 → 18.50 |
| Number Surge + Supply Shortage + Fog | 2/4 → 4/4 | 17.56 → 71.04 | 13.50 → 17.75 |
| High Bounty + Haste + Volatile | 4/4 → 4/4 | 100.00 → 94.63 | 26.00 → 24.50 |
| No Dodge + Fog + Glass Firepower | 4/4 → 4/4 | 72.29 → 65.27 | 24.25 → 24.00 |

Ending-health means include a zero for a fixture that died; they are not survivor-only averages. The two deaths in the first risky mix previously occurred at 18.3 and 34.0 seconds for seed 881; both paired candidate fixtures remained alive to the horizon. This signals that the combined tuning changes affected this controller, not that the mix is now proven fair or easy.

In the High Bounty/Haste/Volatile pairs, scheduled hunters changed from 11 to 6 within the horizon. In the Glass configuration, mean ending health fell despite improved Fog visibility and movement, illustrating why it is useful to check combined effects rather than assume that every change makes the game easier. The baseline's lower kill count also reflects different reinforcement timing.

The arena omits cover, terrain, bosses and extraction routes. The sample has two seeds and one difficulty, and does not cover every modifier combination or a full 4–8 minute raid. **Surviving these fixtures is not a full-raid victory, an extraction success rate, a human win rate or proof that balance is complete.** In particular, 1024 comfort, newcomer understanding and long-run heat decisions remain human-playtest questions.

Full measured fixture records: [v0.11.0 before](evidence/v0111-balance-before.json) and [v0.11.1 after](evidence/v0111-balance-after.json). The diagnostic harness is [tools/balance.mjs](../tools/balance.mjs). Run it without altering a real save:

```bash
node tools/balance.mjs --source /path/to/v0110-snapshot/dist/src --seconds 60 --output /tmp/v0111-balance-before.json
```

## Saves, history and the next decision

The save schema remains **5**, and the desktop app continues using the existing **`number-extraction-v11`** key, application-data profile and internal origin. Upgrading from v0.11.0 does not require a new profile or a new schema/key migration. Existing progress and warehouse ownership continue in place; earlier-version migration and explicit import/export remain available. Finish a raid and close the previous application before launching a new build, since ongoing raids are not restored.

The [v0.11 run-depth document](RUN_DEPTH_V011.md) preserves the original release's design and verification history. The numeric table in this document supersedes its v0.11.0 tuning values for the v0.11.1 candidate. Existing maps, bosses, weapons, enchantments, warehouse rules and privacy requirements are retained.

Next, carry out the [planned anonymous playtest sessions](PLAYTEST_V0111.md), retain actual feedback and tune only where it supports a change. Do not claim that outside players have tested this version or that 2–4 days have passed until those sessions occur. Release verification and human balance evaluation are separate gates.

Phase 2 (v0.12) remains planned: **equipment synergies and set bonuses first, contracts second, factions last**. Proceed after reviewing this tuning pass and its human feedback, not merely because all synthetic fixtures survived.
